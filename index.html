About Arcade Breakout

Arcade Breakout is a classic brick-breaking game that combines simple gameplay with addictive challenges. Maneuver your paddle to bounce the ball and destroy all the bricks on screen. The further you progress, the more challenging the game becomes!

🎮 Engaging Gameplay

Simple controls with challenging brick patterns that keep you coming back for more.

⚡ Dynamic Physics

Realistic ball physics that respond to paddle position for skilled play.
